This guide takes you from a fresh install to media served from the cloud.

:::tip[Before you start]
Create a bucket with your storage provider and get an access key for it. The [provider guides](/providers/) walk you through this for each service.
:::

<Steps>

1. **Choose your provider.**

   Open **OmniOffloader → Settings**. Under **Cloud Provider**, pick your storage provider.

2. **Enter your credentials.**

   Fill in the access key, secret, bucket name and the other fields your provider needs.

   Enter your **Custom Domain / CDN URL** too. This is the public address your media will load from, for example `https://media.yourdomain.com`. Some providers, such as Cloudflare R2, have no public URL until you connect one.

   :::note
   You can keep keys out of the database by defining them in `wp-config.php`. See [Credentials in wp-config.php](/providers/wp-config-credentials/).
   :::

3. **Test the connection.**

   Click **Test Connection**. You should see **Connected**. If not, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting/).

   ![OmniOffloader Provider Settings and Connection Test](/images/screenshots/provider-settings-marked.png)

4. **Choose a retention policy.**

   Start with **Retain Local Files**. It keeps every file on your server while you check that everything loads correctly from the cloud. You can switch to a cleanup policy later and [apply it to existing media](/features/retention-policies/).

5. **Choose a Transfer Speed.**

   **Balanced** is right for most sites. Pick **Low** on a small shared hosting plan. See [Transfer Speed](/features/transfer-speed/).

6. **Save your settings.**

   Click **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the screen.

7. **Offload your existing media.**

   Open **OmniOffloader → Offload** and click **Start Bulk Offload**. The job runs in the background, so you can leave the page. From now on, new uploads are offloaded automatically.

   ![OmniOffloader Bulk Offload Center](/images/screenshots/bulk-offload-marked.png)

8. **Check your site.**

   Open a page with images and check an image address. It should now start with your CDN or custom domain.

</Steps>
